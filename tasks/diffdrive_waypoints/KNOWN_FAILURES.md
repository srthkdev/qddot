`buggy_atan2.py` reverses the bearing arguments, so the base steers away from the first waypoint and collides with arena geometry while correcting. `waypoint_one` fails by about 1.8 s. Use `atan2(dy, dx)`. `buggy_wrap.py` subtracts yaw without wrapping; it takes a long rotation across the ±π boundary and misses `waypoint_three_and_safe` near the end of the 6 s run. Normalize the difference into [-π, π].
